Insights

Wins

  • [Important] Remarketing is the most efficient campaign at 92.4x ROAS and ₹47 per purchase, with the best link CTR in the account (2.70%) on ₹1,367 spend.
  • [Important] 10mm Carbon Belt drove 48 of 89 purchases (54%) and ₹2,35,425 revenue at 89.4x ROAS, the account's volume engine.
  • [Worth doing] Strong mid-funnel: 7.2% of link clicks converted to a purchase, with 1,216 landing page views from 1,240 link clicks (98% load-through).

Concerns

  • [Important] Shaker Bottle trails at 30.8x ROAS and ₹124 per purchase, more than double the account CPA of ₹62. Its link-click to purchase rate is 3.8% against 7.2% for the account, so the leak is on the product page rather than in the ad.
  • [Important] Checkout drop-off: 549 checkouts initiated produced 89 purchases, only 16% completion. Most lost revenue sits between checkout and payment.
  • [Worth doing] Frequency of 3.37 on Remarketing and 3.60 on Carbon Belt within 7 days. Watch for creative fatigue as the audience saturates.
  • [Low priority] Attribution includes 1-day view and engaged-view conversions, so ROAS reads higher than a click-only view. Cross-check against Shopify orders for the same window.

Actions

  • [Urgent] Test a 20–30% budget lift on 10mm Carbon Belt (currently ₹500/day) given 89.4x ROAS and the largest purchase share.
  • [Important] Rework Shaker Bottle: bundle it with a belt or raise AOV with a combo offer, or cap budget until CPA comes down from ₹124.
  • [Important] Audit the checkout step (payment options, shipping cost reveal, COD) and add a checkout-abandoner audience to Remarketing.
  • [Worth doing] Refresh Remarketing creatives before frequency climbs further; 9 of 12 campaigns are dormant with zero spend and can be archived or relaunched with new creative.

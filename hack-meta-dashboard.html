Insights

Wins

  • Important Remarketing returned 109.1x over 30 days (₹6,51,831 on ₹5,977), with 145 purchases at just ₹41 each. It drove 53% of revenue on 31% of spend.
  • Important 10mm Carbon Belt delivered ₹4,14,450 revenue and 93 purchases over 30 days at 47.7x, and stayed the top revenue driver this week (₹1,05,528, 30.3x).
  • Worth doing Shaker Bottle is the most consistent performer: 34.2x over 30 days and 33.7x this week, with steady CPA (₹103 vs ₹112).

Concerns

  • Urgent Remarketing efficiency fell sharply this week: 51.3x vs 109.1x 30-day, CPA up from ₹41 to ₹72, and AOV down from ₹4,495 to ₹3,680. The warm audience (reach 5,636 in 7 days) is likely saturating.
  • Important Carbon Belt CPA climbed to ₹140 this week (vs ₹93 30-day) and link CTR dropped to 0.96% (vs 1.28%), pointing to creative fatigue. It now takes 55% of weekly spend at 30.3x.
  • Important Account-level: 7-day spend per day is up 40% vs the 30-day average, but purchases per day are down 13%. Blended ROAS 35.8x vs 63.2x.

Actions

  • Urgent Refresh Remarketing creatives and add new dynamic product/offer angles. Exclude purchasers from the last 30 days to stop spend on recent buyers and restore the 109x-level return.
  • Important Launch 2 to 3 new Carbon Belt creatives (UGC, lift demos) in the existing ad set to recover CTR before raising its ₹500/day budget.
  • Worth doing Test a modest budget increase on Shaker Bottle (currently ₹200/day) given its stable ~34x ROAS.
  • Low priority 9 campaigns had ₹0 spend in both windows (Gym Bag, Backpack, Pickle Ball Paddle, Signature Belt, Influencer Video and older sales campaigns). Archive or relaunch with fresh creative.

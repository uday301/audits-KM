Insights

What worked, what needs attention, and what to do next.

Wins

  • 10mm Carbon Belt is the revenue engine. ₹4,80,953 on ₹8,586, a 56x return with 125 purchases at ₹69 each. It carries 40% of account revenue and holds a 2.5% link CTR, the strongest cold traffic response in the account.
  • Remarketing returns 71x on a modest budget. ₹5,600 produced ₹4,00,215 and 93 purchases at ₹60, with the highest AOV of the scaled campaigns at ₹4,303. The retargeting pool is converting efficiently and is not yet budget constrained.
  • The account is winning on cheap traffic. A blended CPM of ₹67 and CPC of ₹4.25 across 3,53,766 impressions keeps acquisition cost low, which is what lets a ₹80 cost per purchase sit against a ₹4,039 average order value.
  • Checkout intent is healthy across the account. 1,403 adds to cart and 2,162 checkouts initiated from 5,603 link clicks, so roughly one in four clicks reaches cart. Purchase CVR of 5.3% on link clicks is well ahead of typical sporting goods benchmarks.

Concerns

  • The influencer video variant was paused while returning 94x. It spent only ₹451 and returned ₹42,431 with the highest AOV in the account, ₹6,062, before delivery stopped. This is the single best efficiency in the file and it is currently off.
  • Accessory campaigns buy expensive clicks. Backpack sits at ₹12 CPC and 0.66% CTR, shaker bottle at ₹10 and 0.55%. Both are roughly four times the cost per click of the belt campaigns, so each purchase costs ₹133 and ₹122 against the belt's ₹69.
  • Belt and remarketing frequency is climbing. Carbon Belt is at 5.0 and remarketing at 4.6 impressions per person over 30 days. Creative fatigue usually shows up past 4x, so cost per purchase on these two will start drifting up without a refresh.
  • 7 of 12 campaigns spent nothing. Pickle ball paddle, gym bag, 10mm signature belt and the catalogue and sales campaigns all sat at zero delivery for the full window, so whole product lines received no paid support.

Actions

  • UrgentRestart the influencer video variant with a real budget. It proved ₹42,431 on ₹451. Relaunch at ₹500 per day and watch CPA for the first 72 hours before scaling further.
  • ImportantShift accessory budget into the belt funnel. Moving roughly ₹3,000 of the monthly accessory spend to Carbon Belt and remarketing, at their current returns, is worth around ₹1.5L to ₹2L in incremental revenue against the same money on backpack and shaker.
  • ImportantRefresh creative on the two fatiguing campaigns. Carbon Belt and remarketing both need new hooks before frequency pushes past 5x. Use the influencer format that just delivered 94x as the template.
  • Worth doingRebuild the accessory angle rather than the budget. Backpack and shaker convert well once people click, at 8.7% and 8.1%, so the problem is the creative hook, not the offer. Test bundle framing with a belt purchase.
  • Worth doingDecide on the dormant campaigns. Either fund the 10mm signature belt and gym bag properly or archive them so the account view reflects what is actually running.
  • Low priorityReconcile Meta revenue against Shopify. Reported figures use a 7 day click, 1 day view window, so confirm the 50.4x blended return against backend sales before it goes into a board deck.

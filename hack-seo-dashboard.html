This is the single biggest SEO issue on the account and is directly suppressing rankings and revenue potential on the site's highest-traffic category.

🎯 Actions This Period

  • [Urgent] Immediately resolve the duplicate belt collection URLs by canonicalising /collections/weight-lifting-belts to /collections/weightlifting-belts (or vice versa based on preferred slug), implementing a 301 redirect, and consolidating all internal links and backlinks to the single canonical URL — this will consolidate ranking signals and is projected to meaningfully improve position and CTR on queries collectively receiving 51,000+ impressions/month.

Insights · Wins / Concerns / Actions

🏆 Wins

  • [Important] The homepage and /collections/weightlifting-belts page are standout performers with CTRs of 6.97% and 10.05% respectively, confirming strong brand trust and SERP appeal for users already in the purchase funnel.
  • [Important] Non-branded traffic at 63.8% of clicks demonstrates that Hack Athletics is successfully capturing category-level demand (gym belt, lever belt, hack lever belt), reducing over-reliance on branded queries for organic growth.

⚠️ Concerns

  • [Urgent] The /collections/weight-lifting-belts page receives 40,494 impressions — the highest of any page — but converts at only 1.82% CTR (737 clicks), meaning roughly 39,757 monthly impressions are being wasted. This is the single largest revenue leak in the account.
  • [Urgent] 'gym belt' (9,893 impressions, avg position 7.3, CTR 1.49%) and 'hack lever belt' (9,960 impressions, avg position 3.9, CTR 3.11%) are high-volume queries underperforming on CTR, indicating weak or misaligned title tags and meta descriptions that are failing to capture buyer intent at scale.
  • [Important] The 10mm lever belt product page (/products/hack-athletics-premium-10mm-lever-belt-2-claw) has 4,890 impressions but only 1.98% CTR, suggesting the product listing is surfacing for relevant queries but losing clicks to competitors — likely due to weaker review signals, pricing visibility, or schema markup in SERPs.

🎯 Actions This Period

  • [Urgent] Rewrite the title tag and meta description for /collections/weight-lifting-belts to include a compelling value hook (e.g., price anchor, free shipping, 'India's #1 Lever Belt'). A conservative CTR improvement from 1.82% to 3.5% on 40,494 impressions would yield ~680 additional monthly clicks, potentially adding INR 2,00,000+ in organic revenue at current conversion rates.
  • [Urgent] Target 'gym belt' (position 7.3) with a focused on-page and link-building push to break into the top 5. Add FAQ schema, strengthen internal linking from the homepage and blog to the collection page, and build 3–5 topical supporting articles (e.g., 'How to choose a gym belt') to consolidate topical authority and push this high-volume query into a revenue-driving position.
  • [Important] Implement Product and Review schema markup on both lever belt product pages (10mm and 13mm) to enable star ratings and pricing in SERPs, directly addressing the low CTR problem. Additionally, A/B test product page titles to front-load key differentiators (e.g., thickness, claw count, IPF-spec) that match buyer intent signals from top non-branded queries.

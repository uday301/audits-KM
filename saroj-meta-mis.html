Insights

Read the concerns before the wins. The account is currently not spending.

Wins

  • Remarketing is genuinely profitable. ₹6,292 produced ₹22,857 from 13 orders, a 3.63x return at ₹484 per order against an AOV of ₹1,758.
  • The landing experience holds up. 78.18% of the 715 link clicks became landing page views, which means clicks are arriving rather than bouncing at load. Site speed and link setup are not the problem.
  • The funnel converts at the bottom. 86 adds to cart and 46 checkouts initiated turned into 13 orders, a 28.26% checkout to purchase rate and 15.12% from cart.
  • Women aged 25 to 54 are the buying core. They took ₹4,872 of Remarketing spend and delivered 504 of 715 link clicks, 70.49% of traffic from 77.43% of budget.
  • Best single day was 19 Jul 2026. ₹6,896 of revenue from 4 orders on ₹300 of Remarketing spend, a same day return of 23.02x.

Concerns

  • The account has stopped delivering. Spend fell to ₹42 on 12 Aug 2026 from an average of ₹437 per day, and nothing has run since. Every day blocked is roughly ₹762 of foregone revenue at the current Remarketing rate.
  • Half the budget bought no measurable outcome. Awareness took ₹6,243, or 49.11% of spend, for 1,16,170 impressions, 68 link clicks, zero landing page views and zero orders. It is a Get Directions campaign, so store walk ins are the intended result, but nothing in this account measures them.
  • Remarketing frequency is high and the pool is small. 3.94 impressions per person against a reach of only 5,742. The Dec 2024 website visitor audience is being shown the same single video repeatedly and will fatigue.
  • One ad carries everything. A single creative, the Priyanka HELLO10 video, is the entire sales engine. There is no second ad running, so there is no test and no fallback when it tires.
  • Awareness spend concentrates in one ad. Ad 8 took ₹5,730 of the campaign's ₹6,243, so Ads 4 and 7 never got enough delivery to be judged.
  • Revenue is lumpy. Only 6 of 30 days recorded a purchase, so the 3.63x rests on a thin base of 13 orders and should be treated as directional.

Actions

  • UrgentClear the billing block. Top up the account and check the spend cap, which currently equals amount spent. Nothing else on this list matters until delivery resumes.
  • UrgentRestart Remarketing first. It is the only campaign with proven return. Resume at the ₹200 daily budget, confirm spend for 48 hours, then raise it in steps while watching cost per order against the ₹484 benchmark.
  • ImportantMove the Awareness budget. Shift most of the ₹6,243 into prospecting that can be measured, or into widening the remarketing pool. If the store visit goal is real, it needs offline conversion tracking or a footfall measure before it earns budget again.
  • ImportantRefresh the remarketing audience. Replace the Dec 2024 visitor list with a rolling 30 and 180 day window, and add cart abandoners and content viewers as separate ad sets to pull frequency down from 3.94.
  • Worth doingAdd two creatives against the control. Run a product led cut and an offer led cut alongside the Priyanka video so there is a successor ready before performance decays.
  • Worth doingExtend beyond Stories. All 715 remarketing link clicks came from one placement. Test Reels and Feed inside the sales campaign, where the conversion objective can judge them properly.
  • Low priorityDecide on messaging. Rakshabandhan produced 6 conversations at ₹29 in two days before it stopped. Either commit a real test budget or retire the format.

Insights

What the numbers say and what to do next.

Wins

  • 6.46x ROAS on website visitor remarketing: ₹37,819 back from ₹5,852, well above the 4x scale line.
  • 2.92% link CTR at ₹12.40 per click shows the creative is resonating with warm visitors.
  • 5.72% click-to-purchase rate and 81% of clicks reaching the landing page point to a healthy site experience.
  • CPA of ₹217 is only 15.5% of the ₹1,401 AOV, leaving strong margin headroom.

Concerns

  • UrgentNo prospecting running. 100% of spend is remarketing. Without new top-of-funnel traffic the 5,889-person pool will shrink and results will taper.
  • ImportantSmall, saturating audience. Frequency is 2.74 on 5,889 reach, and the ₹362 CPM reflects bidding on a narrow warm audience.
  • ImportantSingle point of failure. One campaign drives every purchase. Any learning reset, creative fatigue or audience issue stops all sales from Meta.

Actions

  • UrgentLaunch a prospecting Sales campaign (Advantage+ or broad with lookalikes of purchasers) ahead of the Diwali and wedding season demand.
  • ImportantScale the remarketing campaign gradually (15 to 20% every 3 to 4 days) while watching frequency. Pause scaling if it climbs past 4.
  • Worth doingRefresh remarketing creatives with fabric close-ups and new arrivals to hold CTR as frequency builds.
  • Low priorityArchive the 27 dormant campaigns (several are 2020 to 2023 seasonal) to keep the account clean.

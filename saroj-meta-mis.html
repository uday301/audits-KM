Insights · Wins / Concerns / Actions

🏆 Wins

  • [Important] The Remarketing / Website Visitors campaign is the account's one revenue driver — ₹22,857 from ₹6,268 at 3.6x ROAS, 13 purchases and a ₹1,758 AOV. Retargeting is converting profitably.
  • [Worth doing] The Ahmedabad awareness campaign delivered efficient top-funnel reach — 29,320 people and 1,15,888 impressions at a low ₹54 CPM (₹212 per 1,000 reached) for ₹6,229.
  • [Worth doing] The Rakshabandhan messaging campaign opened 6 conversations at just ₹29 each on a tiny ₹173 budget — cheap, warm festive leads.

⚠️ Concerns

  • [Urgent] The account has gone dark — all 3 spending campaigns now show “not delivering” and the other 25 are inactive. Nothing is currently live to capture demand or feed the remarketing pool.
  • [Important] Remarketing efficiency is only modest — 3.6x ROAS and a high ₹482 cost per purchase on a ₹278 CPM. Acceptable for retargeting, but there is no prospecting layer refilling the audience.
  • [Important] Nearly half the spend (₹6,229) went to a pure awareness campaign with no tracked purchases, while no conversion-prospecting campaign was scaled behind it — top-heavy funnel with a weak conversion base.

🎯 Actions This Period

  • [Urgent] Restore delivery — diagnose why the active campaigns flipped to “not delivering” (budget, schedule, or learning-phase exit) and relaunch the remarketing campaign immediately; it is the only proven earner.
  • [Important] Build a prospecting → remarketing funnel — turn on 1–2 conversion prospecting campaigns (Fashion / Cosmetics) to feed the retargeting pool, which will otherwise starve with everything else paused.
  • [Important] Rationalise the account — 25 dormant campaigns (many from 2020–2023) make this unmanageable. Archive the stale ones and consolidate into a lean Prospecting + Remarketing + Catalogue structure.

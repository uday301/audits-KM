Insights

Wins

  • Remarketing earned 6.97x ROAS on Rs 2,898 of spend, well above the 4x scale threshold.
  • Link CTR of 3.30% and a conversion rate of 6.22% of link clicks show warm audiences are responding strongly.
  • Cost per purchase of Rs 207 against an average order value of Rs 1,443 leaves a healthy margin per order.

Concerns

  • [Urgent] All of the revenue (14 purchases) comes from one remarketing campaign. Spend was only Rs 2,898 over 30 days, about Rs 97 a day, so the account has almost no scale.
  • [Important] There is no prospecting or cold audience campaign delivering. Remarketing pools will shrink without new traffic being fed in.
  • [Important] Frequency is 2.28 on a small reach of 2,991, so the remarketing pool is limited and fatigue is a risk if spend is raised without widening the audience.
  • [Low priority] 27 legacy campaigns sit inactive with zero delivery and clutter the account.

Actions

  • [Urgent] Lift the remarketing budget in steps of 20 to 30 percent every 3 to 4 days while watching ROAS stay above 4x and frequency below 3.
  • [Important] Relaunch a sales prospecting campaign (lookalikes of purchasers and the Diwali and festive catalogue) to refill the remarketing audience.
  • [Worth doing] Refresh remarketing creatives with fabric close-ups and new festive collections to protect CTR as frequency climbs.
  • [Low priority] Archive the 27 dormant campaigns so reporting stays clean.

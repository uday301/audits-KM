Insights · Wins / Concerns / Actions

🏆 Wins

  • [Important] The Remarketing / Website Visitors campaign is the account's one revenue driver — ₹13,415 from ₹2,990 at 4.5x ROAS, 8 purchases and a ₹1,677 AOV. Sales ROAS improved from 3.6x last month.
  • [Worth doing] The Ahmedabad awareness campaign delivered broad, efficient reach — 58,337 people and 60,651 impressions at a low ₹50 CPM for ₹3,013.
  • [Worth doing] The Rakshabandhan engagement campaign pulled 64 clicks at a cheap ₹2.8 CPC on a tiny ₹177 festive budget.

⚠️ Concerns

  • [Urgent] Delivery stopped on 12 August — only 16 of 31 days ran. The account has been dark for over two weeks, losing demand capture and letting the remarketing pool go stale.
  • [Important] Remarketing CPA is high at ₹374 on a ₹294 CPM — retargeting a small audience is expensive, and with no prospecting campaign live there is nothing refilling that pool.
  • [Important] Just under half the spend (₹3,013) went to a pure awareness campaign with zero tracked purchases, while no conversion-prospecting campaign was scaled behind it.

🎯 Actions This Period

  • [Urgent] Restart delivery immediately — diagnose why every campaign stopped on 12 Aug (budget exhausted, schedule end, or billing) and relaunch the remarketing campaign, the only proven earner.
  • [Important] Add a prospecting layer — turn on 1–2 conversion prospecting campaigns (Fashion / Cosmetics) to feed the retargeting pool and bring the ₹374 CPA down.
  • [Important] Rebalance the budget — with the account running, tie awareness spend to a conversion campaign rather than letting it absorb half the budget with no tracked return.
